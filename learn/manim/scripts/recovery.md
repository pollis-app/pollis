# Narration script — "Losing a device, and getting back in" (Identity)

No audio yet. Source of truth for the transcript and .vtt. Total ≈ 1:50.

**[00:00–00:25]** Two keys, two jobs. Your PIN unlocks one device, and it is
local to that device; we never see it. Your Secret Key recovers your whole
account. It is shown to you exactly once, and you write it down and keep it safe,
like the deed to a house.

**[00:25–00:55]** Here is how recovery works. The server keeps a copy of your
account key, but it keeps it locked in a box. Only your Secret Key opens that box,
and the server does not have your Secret Key. So the company cannot open your
account, and neither can anyone who steals the box.

**[00:55–01:25]** To sign in on a new device you have two ways. The easy way is to
approve it from a device you already have: the two show the same six-digit code,
you confirm they match, and they agree on a private key in plain sight of the
server. The other way is your Secret Key, which is the same recovery without a
second device. Either way, the new device then joins every group you are in, from
today forward, with no old history, which is the boundary from Topic 4.

**[01:25–01:50]** And one hard rule. If you lose your Secret Key and every device
you were ever signed in on, the account starts fresh. No one holds a spare key to
your account, including us. That is the price of a door only you can open, and it
is the point.
